# World Information

## Key Locations

### The Bakery
- **Location:** North of Main Street
- **NPCs:** Baker (shopkeeper)
- **Menu:**
  1. A danish pastry (7 gold) - Unlimited
  2. A bread (15 gold) - Unlimited
  3. A waybread (76 gold) - Unlimited

### Main Streets of Midgaard
- Multiple "Main Street" locations exist
- The Bakery is accessible from one of them (the western one)
- One Main Street leads south to the Armory
- One Main Street has exits to Market Square (east) and other locations

### Other Notable Locations
- Temple of Midgaard - Starting location
- Temple Square
- Market Square - Central hub
- The Armory - Armor shop south of Main Street
- Common Square - South of Market Square
- Reading Room - West of Temple
- Midgaard Donation Room - East of Temple
- Grunting Boar Inn - East of Temple Square
- Clerics' Guild - West of Temple Square

## Navigation Notes
- From Main Street (Bakery side), going north leads to The Bakery
- From Market Square, going west leads to the correct Main Street for accessing the Bakery
- Multiple exits from each location (n/s/e/w/d)
